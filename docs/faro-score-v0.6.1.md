# FARO Score v0.6.1

FARO Score v0.6.1 is CAPFICO's calibrated public methodology for measuring whether a website is operable for AI Operators.

The full score combines:

- Static Audits: 30%
- Operator Trials: 40%
- Trust Probes: 20%
- Manual Review: 10%

Score bands:

- 92-100: FARO Certified
- 85-91: FARO Ready
- 70-84: Operator-Compatible With Gaps
- 50-69: Operator-Hostile
- 30-49: Not Operable
- 0-29: Invisible to Operators

The Free FARO Scan is an estimate and does not produce a verified score.
