Behaviour, not appearance

We don't just inspect your website.
We put an AI Operator through it.

A real Operator Trial reveals what static checks miss. A page can have products, schema, buttons, policies, and checkout — and still fail when an AI agent tries to use it.

The Full Audit tests whether an Operator can:

  • Understand what your business does.
  • Identify the right product, service, or offer.
  • Extract pricing, availability, shipping, return, and support facts.
  • Detect contradictions instead of blindly trusting the first answer.
  • Traverse checkout, quote, booking, support, or return paths.
  • Use operator-facing surfaces like llms.txt, agent.json, and /.well-known/ucp.
  • Refuse to hallucinate when information is missing.
A website does not become AI-ready because it can be crawled.
It becomes AI-ready when an agent can complete the customer's intent without guessing.
